    Kit list

      Recommendations

      Arrive early

      Gates open at 08:00 and racing starts at 09:10. Traffic on the A43 builds from mid-morning, so aim to be parked by 08:30 and catch the BTCC paddock while it's quiet.

      Sat nav postcode for the circuit is NN12 8TN, but follow event signage for the correct car park.

      Pace the day

      BTCC races run at 11:15, 14:30 and 17:20. Use the gaps for the paddock, food and a move to a new corner, rather than holding one spot all day.

      The pit lane walk and autograph session is at 12:40, straight after the lunch break. Queue early if you want time with the drivers. The best plan is to eat before 12:30 during the Porsche Sprint Challenge.

      Bringing children

      Ear defenders are essential for young children. Touring cars are loud at close range, especially at the start.

      Child tickets are often free or discounted with a paying adult. Check when you book, and pick a base near toilets and food if you have a buggy.

      Follow the action

      Big screens and the circuit commentary cover most viewing areas. Live timing on the official BTCC app or site helps you track pit-free strategy like success ballast and the Race 3 reverse grid.

      Silverstone Museum

      Open 09:00 with last entry at 16:30. Check whether it's included with your ticket. It's a good indoor stop between races if the weather turns or children need a break from the noise.

      Late September weather

      Mornings can be cold and Silverstone is exposed and flat. Layers and a waterproof beat an umbrella, which blocks other people's view.

      Getting away

      BTCC Race 3 finishes around 18:00 and gates close at 18:30. Leaving right after the flag means the worst traffic. Staying for the podium lets the car parks ease.

      Food and cash

      Food outlets get queues around lunch and between races. Eat before the 12:30 lunch break and bring a card; most outlets are cashless.
